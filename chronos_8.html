How to Play

A historical event or artwork appears. Type the year you think it's from and press Enter.

Your year determines where the event lands on the timeline. If it falls between the right neighbours, you score a placement point.

Placed counts correct placements. Years counts how often your year estimate was close to the actual date.

Shortcuts

−44 Minus prefix → automatically switches to before (BCE)
+1503 Plus prefix → automatically switches to after (CE)
Enter Places the event — no button needed
← / → Arrow keys scroll the timeline
